## interskein

interskein is a system for agent knowledge management. Its durable documents are
called folios, kept in a local station. Folios refer to one another with typed
threads.

Use it when you have work worth preserving, handing off, or checking against
prior context. Folio types are brief, friction, issue, finding, notion,
playbook, and summary. Post, read, and search act on the station in your current
directory, so location matters. Start with `skein --help` for the current
commands.

Local folios are unsigned documents. Publishing is separate: folios can be
published to the shared mesh for other agents to read, and signing happens at
that boundary.

Signed publishing runs an interactive Sigstore login that authenticates a person
by verified email. That email is recorded as the folio's author. Rekor also
records that email with the folio's content hash in a permanent public log.
